\section{The Kinds of File and Their Templates}
\label{app:templates}

Table~\ref{tab:kinds} lists the sixteen kinds of file the target metamodel
describes, the template that writes each, and the objects in it. Every file
but the two Vault documents starts with the header line and holds one or more
YAML documents, each after its own \texttt{---} separator.

\begin{table}[!htbp]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.2\linewidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.17\linewidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.55\linewidth}}
\hline
\textbf{Kind (class)} & \textbf{File} & \textbf{Objects written} \\
\hline
\texttt{NamespaceFile} & \path{namespace.yaml} & The project's namespace. \\
\texttt{IndexFile} & \path{kustomization.yaml} & The index of a directory: every file and subdirectory a cluster applies from it; a shared index lists every model's entries (Section~\ref{sec:sharedindex}). \\
\texttt{NetworkPolicyFile} & \path{networkpolicy.yaml} & The project's default-deny policy, or one policy per process with its admitted peers. \\
\texttt{WorkloadFile} & \path{workload.yaml} & One deployment per process: its strategy, security context, containers, sidecars, probes, mounts and volumes. \\
\texttt{SwitchoverFile} & \path{canary.yaml} & One Flagger Canary per blue-green process, its webhooks calling the release gate. \\
\texttt{ServiceFile} & \path{service.yaml} & One service per other process that serves a port. \\
\texttt{IdentityFile} & \path{serviceaccount.yaml} & Each process's service account, and its backup identity's. \\
\texttt{ClaimFile} & \path{pvc.yaml} & A claim per volume, and per backed-up volume the claim its copies land on, kept from pruning. \\
\texttt{ConfigMapFile} & \path{configmap.yaml} & An immutable configuration map per configuration file, its text as a block scalar. \\
\texttt{BackupFile} & \path{backup.yaml} & A scheduled backup job per backed-up volume. \\
\texttt{MonitorFile} & \path{podmonitor.yaml} or \path{servicemonitor.yaml} & The monitor of a scraped application. \\
\texttt{SecretFile} & \path{vso.yaml} & Per identity holding a secret: how the operator authenticates as it, and one synchronised secret per grant. \\
\texttt{ConnectionFile} & \path{vaultconnection.yaml} & The secret operator's connection to the secret store. \\
\texttt{IngressRouteFile} & \path{<application>-<exposure>.yaml} & A Traefik route per exposure, in its entry point's shared directory. \\
\texttt{VaultPolicyFile} & \path{<identity>.policy.json} & The Vault policy an identity's grants derive, as JSON. \\
\texttt{VaultRoleFile} & \path{<identity>.role.json} & The Kubernetes authentication role that binds the identity to its policy, as JSON. \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\caption{The Kinds of File}
\label{tab:kinds}
\end{table}
