\subsection{Continuous and Interrupted Applications in One Project}
\label{sec:appendix-knowledge}

The input is \texttt{examples/knowledge/knowledge.project.yml}, with the two
applications of Table~\ref{tab:examples}. In the resolved model, the
\texttt{continuous} API requires a release gate and its declared migration. The
\texttt{interrupted} worker has neither: it declares no migration, and its old
version stops before the new one starts
(Section~\ref{sec:targetmetamodel}).

\subsection{A Rejected Model}
\label{sec:appendix-refused}

\texttt{refusals/prepare-process-serves.project.yml} declares a prepare process
with a port, probes, replicas and a \texttt{cutover}. These belong to serving
processes. Its XMI is \texttt{prepare-process-serves.project.xmi}.
Validating that XMI reports \texttt{E\_PREPARE\_PROCESS\_SERVES} on the second
process of the first application.

\subsection{The Hand-in Archive}
\label{sec:handin}

The archive holds this report as \texttt{report.pdf}, a \texttt{README.md}
with the steps below, and two folders. The first, \texttt{task-1-metamodelling/}, is an
Eclipse project with OCL and Xtext natures, imported with File > Import >
Existing Projects into Workspace. It contains the metamodels, five accepted
examples and 33 rejected models: 18 single files and 15 document sets.
\texttt{implementation/} contains the EMF implementation
(Section~\ref{sec:implementation}) and the examples its tests read.
Table~\ref{tab:handin} gives the layout. Each authored YAML file appears beside
its parsed XMI, with any referenced \texttt{env/} files.

Each XMI declares its metamodel in \texttt{xsi:schemaLocation}, allowing Eclipse
to open it without a registered package. The archive's
\texttt{project-intent.ecore} embeds the OCL invariants and helpers. Plain
Validate reports single-document codes. For cross-document sets, open the
project XMI, add the platform XMI with Load Resource, then select OCL > Load
Document and load \texttt{model/project-intent.ocl} before validating. Only
\texttt{minimal} includes a resolved XMI. Task 2 will produce the others.

\texttt{./mvnw verify} in \texttt{implementation/emf} builds the implementation
and runs its tests; it needs JDK~21. For each example and rejected model it
writes the parsed model or the diagnostics, and the XMI, under
\texttt{bundles/cli/}\allowbreak\texttt{target/parity/}. To open the implementation in Eclipse,
import \texttt{implementation/emf} with File > Import > Existing Maven Projects
after setting \texttt{emf.target} as the target platform.

\section{All Constraints}
\label{sec:allconstraints}

Table~\ref{tab:constraints} lists each invariant of
Section~\ref{sec:constraints} by the code it reports, with its context class.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\footnotesize
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{0.8}
\begin{tabular}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.39\linewidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.14\linewidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.39\linewidth}}
\hline
\textbf{Code} & \textbf{Context} & \textbf{What it rejects} \\
\hline
E\_ALERT\_CLASS\_WITHOUT\_SIGNAL & Observability & An alert level is set, but no metrics are collected. \\
E\_CUTOVER\_UNHONOURABLE & Process & A \texttt{continuous} process whose storage cannot attach to the second copy a blue/green switch starts. \\
E\_CUTOVER\_MISSING & Process & A process with no \texttt{cutover} value at any level. A prepare process is exempt, because it replaces no running version. \\
E\_RELEASE\_UNIT\_MIXED\_CUTOVER & Application & An application whose processes have different \texttt{cutover} values, although it switches as one unit. \\
E\_PREPARE\_PROCESS\_SERVES & Process & A prepare process with a port, probes, replicas or a \texttt{cutover}, which only a serving process has. \\
E\_SHARED\_DECLARATION\_DUPLICATED & seven classes & A shared declaration repeated unchanged at a lower level, where it has no effect. \\
E\_SHARED\_QUANTITY & Placement & A memory or CPU quantity declared above process level, where it would mean something different for each process. \\
E\_PLACEMENT\_INCOMPLETE & Process & A process with no memory or CPU quantity of its own. No higher level may supply one. \\
E\_OWNER\_ROLE\_GRANTED & DatabaseGrant & A hand-written grant of the database role that changes the schema. The platform derives this role for the migration only. \\
E\_MIGRATION\_OWNER\_DUPLICATED & Migration & A second application in one project that declares a migration for the project's database. \\
E\_ENGINE\_WITHOUT\_DURABILITY & Process & A backup engine declared where no volume's durability class requires a backup. \\
E\_DURABILITY\_WITHOUT\_ENGINE & Volume & A volume that requires a backup, with no engine to state the backup method. \\
E\_ENV\_CANNOT\_RELOAD & Rotation & A secret that tolerates a reload but is delivered as an environment variable, which is read only at start. \\
E\_ILLEGAL\_DELIVERY\_FOR\_ACCESS & KvGrant & \texttt{custody} delivered as an environment variable or file, although its paths appear only at runtime. Also \texttt{self-renew} delivered as an environment variable, which its identity cannot read. \\
E\_NON\_KV\_DELIVERY & TransitGrant, DatabaseGrant & A transit or database grant delivered as an environment variable or file. The application requests transit operations from the secret store and fetches database credentials issued per lease. \\
E\_DUPLICATE\_ROUTE\_MATCH & Route & Two routes of one exposure with the same path and match type, which the derived precedence cannot order. \\
E\_NO\_FORWARD\_AUTH\_ENDPOINT & Tier & A tier that serves authenticated visitors but declares no endpoint to check whether a visitor may enter. \\
E\_HANDOVER\_BOTH\_PATHS & HandoverLedger & A project listed on both delivery paths, so two sources would both apply it. \\
E\_NO\_TIER\_FOR\_AUDIENCE & Exposure, Route & An audience no declared edge tier serves, so the route reaches no edge. \\
E\_NO\_ENGINE\_POLICY & Process & An engine with no backup method in the platform document. \\
E\_NO\_DURABILITY\_POLICY & Volume & A durability class with no policy in the platform document. \\
E\_SECRETS\_AT\_REST\_REQUIRED & Grant & A secret written into a cluster that does not encrypt secrets at rest. \\
E\_MIGRATION\_UNDECLARED & Application & An application that uses a database but declares no migration. \\
E\_MIGRATION\_WITHOUT\_DATABASE & Migration & A migration on an application that uses no database, checked only when the documents of all its providers are loaded. \\
E\_CREDENTIALS\_WITHOUT\_DATABASE & Credentials & A rotation override on a dependency whose provider has no database. \\
E\_NO\_MIGRATION\_POLICY & Migration & A changelog, where the platform document defines no migration runner to build it on. \\
E\_NO\_DELIVERY\_POLICY & Application & A \texttt{continuous} application, where the platform document defines no check interval for its blue/green switch. \\
E\_HANDOVER\_UNLISTED & Project & A project that the handover ledger lists on neither delivery path. \\
\hline
